TRAVEL SMART

Philippines Travel Guide

Everything you need to know before exploring the beautiful islands of the Philippines.

Travel Essentials

Follow these simple tips for a safe and enjoyable trip.

Packing Checklist

✔ Lightweight clothing
✔ Sunscreen
✔ Valid ID
✔ Water bottle
✔ Camera
✔ Power bank

Budget Tips

Travel during the off-season, book accommodations early, use public transportation, and enjoy affordable local food.

Transportation

Travel by airplane, ferry, bus, jeepney, tricycle, or train depending on your destination.

Safety Reminders

Stay Safe

Always follow local regulations, keep emergency contacts available, and protect your personal belongings.

Best Time to Visit

The dry season from November to May offers the best weather for beaches, island hopping, and sightseeing.

Protect Nature

Dispose of waste properly, avoid damaging coral reefs, and respect wildlife and local communities.

Emergency Contacts

Keep these important numbers available while traveling.

National Emergency

911
For police, fire, and medical emergencies anywhere in the Philippines.

Medical Assistance

Locate the nearest hospital or health center when visiting a new destination.

Tourist Assistance

Visit the local tourism office for maps, recommendations, and travel information.

Travel Do's & Don'ts

Be a responsible traveler and help preserve the beauty of the Philippines.

Do

✔ Respect local traditions.
✔ Support local businesses.
✔ Keep beaches and parks clean.
✔ Follow safety signs.
✔ Stay hydrated.

Don't

✘ Leave trash behind.
✘ Damage wildlife or coral reefs.
✘ Ignore local rules.
✘ Feed wild animals.
✘ Enter restricted areas.
