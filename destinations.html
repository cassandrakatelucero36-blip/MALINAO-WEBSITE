Palawan

Famous for El Nido, Coron, crystal-clear lagoons, and limestone cliffs.

Home of the world-famous White Beach and stunning sunsets.

Siargao

The surfing capital of the Philippines with beautiful island hopping.

Bohol

Chocolate Hills, tarsiers, beaches, and river cruises.

Mayon Volcano

Known for its perfect cone shape and spectacular scenery.

Banaue Rice Terraces

A UNESCO-recognized masterpiece carved into the mountains.

Cebu City

Baguio City

Vigan

Intramuros

Explore the historic Walled City and centuries-old churches in Manila.

Chocolate Hills

One of the Philippines' most famous natural wonders with over 1,200 unique hills.

Davao City

Home of Mount Apo, Philippine Eagle Center, and fresh tropical fruits.
